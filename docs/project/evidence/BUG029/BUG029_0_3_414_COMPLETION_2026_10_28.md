## Defect, fix, and boundaries

BUG029 was exposed by unpublished I130-B TLS composition: an outer `BufferedReader` read cancelled after selection but before its deferred C′ work began could retain `activeReq` indefinitely and prevent queued followers from reaching the source. The I130-B focal observation was 63/64 passing with `ProtosTlsClientIntegrationTest.aCancelledReadLosesNoCiphertextOrPlaintext` pending on the second read; that historical result is **not** a post-BUG029 TLS retest.

The published shared-runtime repair:

- Adds `ProtosIoOperation.onDeferredCPrimeTerminalIdleForRuntime`, a one-shot runtime-internal callback claimed under `deferredCPrimeLock` and delivered outside locks only when the operation is terminal and no deferred C′ segment is running. A callback failure does not skip Actor-domain or lifecycle deregistration.
- Registers the notification before C′ scheduling; handles cancellation racing installation/request of deferred execution; terminal requests are retired idempotently while genuine live scheduling defects still fail/propagate.
- Makes `BufferedReader` request retirement once-only through `Req.retired`. When no lower read was installed it frees the active position and pumps followers; a pending lower Future **retains its position** until terminal, preventing later reads from overtaking possible lower consumption.
- Leaves Future commitment/cancellation authority and public I/O semantics unchanged; does not create a Task, scheduler or second operation owner.

The product commit adds **14 Java tests** across two existing suites:

- **BufferedReader C′ (6):** `cancelBeforeFirstCPrimeDispatchRetiresActiveReadAndUnblocksNextRead`, `cancelActiveReadReleasesAlreadyQueuedFollowersInOrder`, `cancelQueuedMiddleReadPreservesRelativeOrderOfOthers`, `cancelDuringSuspendedGuestTargetReadRetiresWithoutLateResume`, `cancelAfterLowerReadStartedKeepsFollowerBehindPendingLower`, `closeBeforeFirstCPrimeDispatchFailsActiveAndQueuedReadsWithoutTargetRead`.
- **Non-Task C′ operation ownership (8):** `terminalIdleIsDeliveredOnceForCancellationBeforeFirstDispatch`, `terminalIdleRegisteredAfterTerminalityIsDeliveredImmediatelyOnce`, `terminalIdleFollowsCancellationOfSuspendedCPrimeWithoutGuestResume`, `terminalIdleWaitsForRunningCPrimeSegmentToExit`, `repeatedCancellationAndTerminalRequestsDeliverNoAdditionalNotification`, `terminalIdleDoesNotAlterNormalCompletionOrCommitment`, `terminalIdleCallbackFailureStillRetiresOperationFromLifecycle`, `terminalIdleWaitsForConcurrentRunningSegmentToExit`.

The after-lower-start cancellation test uses a **genuinely cancelled** lower Future before any bytes are obtained, avoiding confusion with the separate preservation-of-late-bytes defect.
