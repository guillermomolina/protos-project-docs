## Purpose

TEST006 was allocated to separate a release-admission policy question from the
technical defect tracked by BUG013 and the concrete publication flow tracked by
DIST009.

The immediate question is whether a known upstream Native guest runtime-compiler
bailout, with correct guest fallback and no established observable Protos
semantic failure, must remain release-blocking for the `JVM_PLUS_NATIVE`
artifact model.

This record is a routing checkpoint. It does not decide that question and does
not authorize weakening the maintained Native gate.

## Historical admission distinction

Earlier Protos Native validation used a weaker forced-JIT workload. Retained
BUG013 evidence later showed that a historically green probe could obtain
successful Tier-2 compilations and still encounter later hot recompilation
failures when exercised with a stronger workload.

That means the current blocker cannot be interpreted simply as "Native Image no
longer builds" or "the Native executable cannot run". The current question is
narrower: whether the strengthened requirement that no hot guest compilation
attempt fail is itself a release correctness invariant.

Published Native artifacts predating the hardened gate are therefore relevant
historical evidence for TEST006, but their existence alone is not proof that the
upstream bailout is harmless.
