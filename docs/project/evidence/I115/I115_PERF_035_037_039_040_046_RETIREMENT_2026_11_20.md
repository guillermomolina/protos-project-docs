# I115 — Closure of five superseded PERF work items (2026-11-20)

**Role:** Revision-bound, non-normative project coordination and evidence. This records an explicit maintainer decision to stop treating five formerly independent performance work items as separate implementation campaigns after completion of I115. It does **not** silently mark unmet case-specific benchmarks, graph targets, peer-language comparisons, or language-family semantic matrices as verified.

## Owner authorization and lifecycle disposition

The maintainer instructed (2026-11-20): **"de los perf, salvo perf044 y perf045 el resto se pueden cerrar, han quedado implementados en I115"**.

The seven then-open `family:PERF` issues were examined in GitHub. The **five explicitly selected for closure** are:

**Closure code**: `closed / not_planned` means *independent scope superseded by I115*, **not** abandoned optimization source, incomplete language behavior accepted as correct, or achievement of every original checklist. Original issue bodies and historical failures/constraints remain preserved. No new PERF issue is allocated.
