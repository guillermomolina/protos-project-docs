The published commit changes exactly eight product paths:

1. `src/main/java/com/guillermomolina/protos/execution/host/tls/ProtosTlsFailure.java` — conclusive private `RECORD_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` fact.
2. `src/main/java/com/guillermomolina/protos/execution/host/tls/ProtosTlsClientEngine.java` — 5-octet inbound TLS record header check against **18,432-byte encrypted fragment bound**, on the actual current buffer position after `flip()`, before each `SSLEngine.unwrap`, preserving `compact()` and sticky failure semantics; ignores unrecognized content-type/version headers and leaves general TLS validation to JSSE.
3. `protos/lib/tls/internal/Errors.protos` — maps the private fact to fresh public `TlsError.kind == "limit"` during and after handshake; all other fallbacks remain phase-dependent.
4. `protos/lib/tls/Client.protos` — corrects commentary to the actually published temporary NR-LDH restriction; `xn--` A-labels are not yet accepted and `IpAddress` remains supported.
5. `src/test/java/com/guillermomolina/protos/execution/host/tls/ProtosTlsClientEngineTest.java` — fragmented header, inclusive boundary, unknown/non-TLS input, handshake/post-handshake, coalesced records and valid fragmented ciphertext regressions.
6. `protos/tests/library/tls/client-handshake.protos` — guest mapping, fragmented oversized record and owning cleanup regressions.
7. `pom.xml` — published implementation-version finalization.
8. `CHANGELOG.md` — published release entry.

No normative `spec/io/TLS.md` or `spec/PROTOS_SPEC_CHANGELOG.md` amendment was made for C1. This hardening implements the already selected spec, not a new policy.
