## Defect, fix, and boundaries

[BUG029 #980](https://github.com/guillermomolina/protos/issues/980) addressed a different pre-start cancellation queue blockage. BUG030 addressed the remaining data-conservation violation: after a lower `read` was invoked, the outer `BufferedReader` Future could become cancelled and the lower Future could nevertheless resolve with bytes already obtained. The former terminal-outer early return in `settleReadLower` retired the request before inspecting those bytes, dropping them from the observable input sequence, contrary to `BYTE_IO.md` §5.1.

The published shared-runtime repair:

- Handles terminal lower `RESOLVED` outcomes even if the outer read is already terminal. If the outer `commit()` cannot succeed, nonempty lower `Bytes` are appended to the adapter input while its lifecycle remains `OPEN`, preserving logical order for subsequent reads; the cancelled outer read is not revived and does not extract data.
- Separates lower `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`, EOF, malformed and empty results. Lower failure/cancellation cannot overwrite a terminal outer outcome, and a lower cancellation that yields no bytes does not fabricate input. Closed lifecycle state does not retain new buffered bytes.
- Serializes C′ lower settlement with a per-request monitor-protected `lowerSettling` claim so the lower observer and resumed C′ continuation cannot both settle the same result.
- Retains an active request and its lower observer while a lower read is pending after outer cancellation, then retires it through the shared settlement path. Requests without a lower read still retire promptly under BUG029; followers do not overtake pending lower consumption.
- Preserves existing operation commitment, cancellation authority, queue/FIFO and non-Task C′ ownership; no new Task or alternative scheduler is introduced.

The product commit changes **one historical C′ test** from expecting discarded late bytes to expecting their preservation (`cancelledBufferedReadPreservesLateReadAheadAndUnblocksNextRead`) and adds **nine Java tests** in two existing suites:

- **C′ (five new):** `cancelAfterLowerResolutionBeforeCPrimeResumePreservesBytes`, `cancelledLowerReadWithoutBytesAllowsNextRead`, `queuedFollowerReceivesLateCancelledReadBytesBeforeNewLowerData`, `queuedCancellationDoesNotDiscardPreviouslyBufferedPrefix`, `lowerResolutionDuringCancellationRequestPreservesBytesExactlyOnce`.
- **Legacy observer (four new):** `cancelledLegacyReadPreservesLateResolvedBytes`, `cancelledLegacyLowerWithoutBytesDoesNotFabricateInput`, `legacyLowerResolutionRacingOuterCancellationPreservesBytes`, `committedLegacyReadCannotBeRewrittenByCancellation`.

The regression coverage includes late read-ahead ordering (`Z`, `W`, then `Y` with exactly two source reads), no-byte lower cancellation, queued followers, previously buffered prefixes, cancellation/reentrant lower resolution, race capture and irreversible committed results. It does not assert a result for unpublished TLS acceptance tests.
