## Exact owner approval

Active interaction, 2026-11-26: “apruebo D270-D1-B.” The owner separately authorized updating Issues and bounded direct publication in this repository. This explicitly selects D1-B over D1-A, on top of earlier D270-D approval. It does not authorize an unpresented design, product repository publication, or an assertion that validation was performed.

D1-A rejected: fail every write still pending when abort is invoked, even if its previously admitted final attempt actually establishes success. D1-B selected: an attempt already admitted before the irreversible abort cutover may finish; when it establishes the complete successful effect under the operation contract, preserve success even if publication follows cutover. Preserve independently established errors and committed results; if success cannot be established, report the appropriate failure. No new effect attempt may be admitted after cutover, including attempts continuing a previously committed prefix. An in-flight attempt is not evidence of zero effect or of full success. Reconcile actual/unknown contribution without rollback, fabricated prefix counts, automatic retry or a new Future state.

Example: 100-byte write, 60 committed, a 25-byte attempt admitted before cutover, 15 not yet admitted. Reconcile the 25-byte attempt; do not start the last 15. The write fails with IOError if incomplete after commitment. If an already admitted final attempt instead completes all remaining bytes and establishes success, preserve the successful result. A merely delayed publication is not a reason to fail. A failure independently established before/through that attempt retains its contract.
